# UCC in Daily Life

A no-build, static training app on negotiable instruments (UCC Articles 3 & 4) for everyday use, with cross-references to Black's Law Dictionary terms (paraphrased) and federal statutes/regulations.

- Part 1: 11 scenario-based lessons + quizzes (checks, indorsements, HDC, notes, cashier's checks, stop payments, forgery, scams, discharge)
- Part 2: What you were taught vs. how money works (sourced) + myth-vs-fact
- Part 3: Debt — public vs. private, HJR-192 / 31 U.S.C. § 5118, 12 U.S.C. § 411, lawful discharge, case law
- Tools: negotiability checker, indorsement builder, loss-allocation tree, statute-of-limitations estimator, stop-payment tracker
- Flashcards and a searchable cross-reference index

Run: `python3 -m http.server 8080` in this folder and open http://localhost:8080. Content lives in `data/*.js`.

Educational content only — not legal advice.
